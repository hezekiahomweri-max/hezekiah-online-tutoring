Acids and Bases

Concise revision notes for Grade 10 learners covering properties, indicators, pH, reactions, strength, electrical conductivity and common gas tests.

Grade 10 Chemistry BooksFree Revision PDFs

1. What are acids and bases?

An acid produces hydrogen ions, H⁺(aq), in aqueous solution. A base reacts with an acid to form salt and water. A soluble base is called an alkali and produces hydroxide ions, OH⁻(aq), in aqueous solution.

REMEMBER: Acids provide H⁺(aq) ions while alkalis provide OH⁻(aq) ions in water.

2. Indicators and pH

IndicatorAcidAlkali
Blue/red litmusBlue litmus turns redRed litmus turns blue
Methyl orangeRedYellow
PhenolphthaleinColourlessPink

The pH scale indicates how acidic or alkaline an aqueous solution is. Values below 7 are acidic, pH 7 is neutral, and values above 7 are alkaline.

3. Typical reactions of acids

Acid + metal → salt + hydrogen gas

Mg(s) + 2HCl(aq) → MgCl₂(aq) + H₂(g)

Acid + base → salt + water

HCl(aq) + NaOH(aq) → NaCl(aq) + H₂O(l)

Acid + carbonate → salt + water + carbon dioxide

2HCl(aq) + CaCO₃(s) → CaCl₂(aq) + H₂O(l) + CO₂(g)

4. Strong and weak acids and bases

Strength describes the extent to which an acid or base ionises in water. A strong acid ionises to a greater extent than a weak acid. Strength should not be confused with concentration, which describes the amount of solute in a given volume of solution.

EXAM FOCUS: Do not use “strong” and “concentrated” as if they mean the same thing.

5. Electrical conductivity

Aqueous acids and alkalis conduct electricity because they contain mobile ions. Differences in ion concentration can affect the observed conductivity.

6. Gas tests in acid reactions

Hydrogen: gives a characteristic pop with a lighted splint. Carbon dioxide: turns limewater milky.

Quick Check

  1. State the ion responsible for acidic properties in aqueous solutions.
  2. What colour does phenolphthalein show in an alkaline solution?
  3. Write a word equation for the reaction between an acid and a carbonate.
  4. Explain why aqueous acids conduct electricity.
  5. Distinguish between acid strength and acid concentration.

Application Challenge

A learner is given two colourless acidic solutions of equal concentration. Solution A produces a higher electrical conductivity reading than solution B. Suggest what this observation may indicate about the extent of ionisation of the two acids.
