Free practice: Finding protons, neutrons and electrons

Atomic number Z gives the proton number. Mass number A gives protons plus neutrons, so neutrons = A − Z. In a neutral atom, electron number equals proton number.

Worked example

An atom has Z = 12 and A = 24. It has 12 protons, 24 − 12 = 12 neutrons and, if neutral, 12 electrons. Its electron arrangement is 2, 8, 2 in the shell model used here.

Try it yourself

Attempt the questions before opening the answers.

  1. A neutral atom has Z = 8 and A = 16. State its proton, neutron and electron numbers.
  2. A neutral atom has 11 protons and 12 neutrons. Find its mass number.
  3. A wave has frequency 5 Hz and wavelength 2 m. Find its speed.
Show answers and working
  1. 8 protons, 16 − 8 = 8 neutrons and 8 electrons.
  2. A = 11 + 12 = 23.
  3. v = fλ = 5 × 2 = 10 m/s.

Remember: Mass number is a count of particles in one nucleus; it is not the same quantity as relative atomic mass.
