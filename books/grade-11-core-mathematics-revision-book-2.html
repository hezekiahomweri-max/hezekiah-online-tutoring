Free practice: Simplifying and combining surds

Extract square factors first. Then combine only terms with the same irrational factor; √2 and √3 cannot be added as like terms.

Worked example

Simplify 2√12 + √27 − √3. Since √12 = 2√3 and √27 = 3√3, the expression becomes 4√3 + 3√3 − √3 = 6√3.
