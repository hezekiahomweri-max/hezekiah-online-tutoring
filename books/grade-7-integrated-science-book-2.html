Free practice: Identifying variables in an investigation

Change the independent variable, measure the dependent variable and keep relevant control variables the same. This helps make a comparison fair.

Worked example

To investigate how the amount of water affects plant growth, change the amount of water and measure the increase in plant height over the same period. Keep plant type, soil, light, container size and starting size comparable.

Try it yourself

Attempt the questions before opening the answers.

  1. What is the independent variable in the example?
  2. What is the dependent variable?
  3. Name two factors to keep comparable.
Show answers and working
  1. The amount of water supplied.
  2. Plant growth, measured here as the increase in height.
  3. Any two: plant type, soil, light, container size, starting size or observation period.

Remember: An unusual reading should be investigated and checked, rather than deleted simply because it does not match a prediction.
