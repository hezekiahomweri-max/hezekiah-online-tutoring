Free practice: Applying the equation of continuity

For steady incompressible flow through a pipe with no leaks, A₁v₁ = A₂v₂. A narrower section has a higher flow speed under these conditions.

Worked example

Water flows at 2 m/s through a pipe area of 0.006 m². The volume flow rate is Q = Av = 0.012 m³/s. If the next section has area 0.003 m², v = Q/A = 4 m/s.

Try it yourself

Show your working or explain your reasoning, then open the answers to check.

  1. Find Q when A = 0.004 m² and v = 3 m/s.
  2. For steady incompressible flow, what happens to speed if pipe area halves?
  3. A vehicle accelerates from 5 m/s to 17 m/s in 4 s. Find its average acceleration.
Show answers and working
  1. Q = Av = 0.012 m³/s.
  2. Speed doubles, because Av remains constant.
  3. a = (17 − 5)/4 = 3 m/s².

Remember: Continuity is a statement about conserved flow. Identify the flow assumptions before applying the equation.
