Free practice: Subtracting a negative integer

Subtracting a negative number has the same effect as adding the corresponding positive number. A number line can help you check the direction of the change.

Worked example

Evaluate −6 − (−9). Change subtraction of −9 into addition of 9: −6 + 9 = 3. On a number line, start at −6 and move nine places to the right.

Try it yourself

Show your working or explain your reasoning, then open the answers to check.

  1. Evaluate −12 + 7.
  2. Temperature rises from −3 °C to 5 °C. Find the rise.
  3. Calculate 3/4 − 1/6.
Show answers and working
  1. −5.
  2. 5 − (−3) = 8 °C.
  3. 9/12 − 2/12 = 7/12.

Remember: The sign belongs to the number. Keep brackets when subtracting a negative value to make each operation clear.
