Free practice: Using a fraction of a quantity

To find a fraction of a whole quantity, divide by the denominator and multiply by the numerator. This helps connect the calculation to equal groups.

Worked example

A school has 640 learners and 3/8 take part in a club. One eighth is 640 ÷ 8 = 80 learners, so three eighths is 80 × 3 = 240 learners.

Try it yourself

Show your working or explain your reasoning, then open the answers to check.

  1. There are 24 boxes with 18 books in each. Find the total number of books.
  2. Find 0.75 + 1.6.
  3. A square has area 81 m². Find its perimeter.
Show answers and working
  1. 24 × 18 = 432 books.
  2. Align decimal points: 0.75 + 1.60 = 2.35.
  3. Side = √81 = 9 m. Perimeter = 4 × 9 = 36 m.

Remember: An area is measured in square units; a perimeter is measured in length units. State the appropriate unit with your answer.
