Free practice: Dividing by a fraction

To divide by a non-zero fraction, multiply by its reciprocal. Simplify before multiplying when possible, then judge whether the answer is reasonable.

Worked example

Calculate 3/4 ÷ 3/8. Rewrite as 3/4 × 8/3 = 2. Two portions of size 3/8 fit into 3/4, which explains the answer.

Try it yourself

Show your working or explain your reasoning, then open the answers to check.

  1. Calculate 5/6 ÷ 5/12.
  2. Find 2/5 of KSh 1,500.
  3. Evaluate (−8) × (−3) − 5.
Show answers and working
  1. 5/6 × 12/5 = 2.
  2. 1,500 ÷ 5 × 2 = KSh 600.
  3. 24 − 5 = 19.

Remember: Multiplying by the reciprocal applies to division. Do not reverse the second fraction when the original operation is addition.
