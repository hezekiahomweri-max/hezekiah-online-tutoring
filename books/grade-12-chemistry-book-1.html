Free practice: Explaining the Contact Process

In the Contact Process, sulfur dioxide reacts with oxygen to form sulfur trioxide. Explain a condition in terms of reaction rate, equilibrium yield or practical cost, rather than listing it without a reason.

Worked example

The central reaction is 2SO₂ + O₂ ⇌ 2SO₃. A catalyst speeds up the approach to equilibrium without changing the equilibrium yield. Industrial choices must balance useful production rate with achievable yield and operating costs.

Try it yourself

Show your working or explain your reasoning, then open the answers to check.

  1. Balance SO₂ + O₂ → SO₃.
  2. Name the three principal products of the chlor-alkali process using aqueous sodium chloride.
  3. In iron extraction, what is the role of limestone?
Show answers and working
  1. 2SO₂ + O₂ → 2SO₃.
  2. Chlorine, hydrogen and sodium hydroxide.
  3. It decomposes to calcium oxide, which removes silica impurity by forming slag. Limestone is not the reducing agent.

Remember: A catalyst changes reaction speed, not the equilibrium position. Distinguish its role from the effects of temperature and pressure.
