Topical Practice Sample

2. Explain why a sodium ion is smaller than a sodium atom.

Application & Practical Sample

A learner adds equal pieces of magnesium ribbon to equal volumes of two hydrochloric acid solutions of different concentration.

(a) State the independent variable.
(b) State one controlled variable.
(c) State one observation that can be used to compare reaction rates.
(d) Explain why repeating the experiment improves reliability.

National Examination Practice Sample

A water sample has pH 2 and reacts with magnesium to produce a gas that burns with a squeaky pop.

(a) What does pH 2 indicate?
(b) Identify the gas produced.
(c) Explain why these observations alone do not prove that the acid is hydrochloric acid.
(d) State an additional test that could provide evidence for chloride ions.

EXAM FOCUS: Give conclusions only when the evidence supports them.
