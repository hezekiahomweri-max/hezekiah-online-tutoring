Atomic Structure Preview

Atomic number is the number of protons in the nucleus. Mass number is the total number of protons and neutrons. Isotopes are atoms of the same element with the same number of protons but different numbers of neutrons.

REMEMBER: For a neutral atom, number of protons = number of electrons.

Quick Check: An atom contains 12 protons, 13 neutrons and 12 electrons. State its atomic number and mass number.

Acids and Bases Preview

Acids react with some metals to form a salt and hydrogen gas. Example: Mg + 2HCl → MgCl₂ + H₂. Carbonates react with acids to form a salt, water and carbon dioxide.

Gas tests: Hydrogen gives a squeaky pop with a lighted splint. Carbon dioxide turns limewater milky.

APPLICATION: Explain why a carbonate rock produces effervescence when dilute acid is added.

Practical Reasoning Preview

A good investigation identifies the independent variable, dependent variable and controlled variables. Scientific reasoning follows: Observation → Interpretation → Conclusion.

THINK BIG: Suggest one way to improve the reliability of an experiment measuring the rate of an acid–metal reaction.
