Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Parents & Learners Often Ask

Are the lessons individual or group-based?

Both options are available. Individual tutoring provides personalized support, while small-group revision offers collaborative practice at a lower per-student rate.

Which online platforms are used?

Lessons can be conducted through Google Meet or Zoom, depending on the agreed arrangement.

Can a learner receive help with a specific difficult topic?

Yes. Sessions can focus on particular concepts, homework, revision gaps or problem-solving skills rather than following a fixed sequence every time.

Do you guarantee a particular grade?

No. The academy provides professional teaching, practice and revision support, but academic outcomes also depend on the learner's effort, attendance, practice and other factors.

Will learning materials be available for purchase?

The Learning Resource Centre is being developed. Original notes, worksheets, topical practice and revision packs will be introduced progressively.

How do I book?

The quickest option is WhatsApp. You can also call, send an SMS or email using the contact details below.
