Why Peak Achievers?

Understand. Practise. Build confidence.

Every learner has different strengths, gaps and goals. Lessons are structured to make difficult ideas clearer and revision more manageable.

01

Concept Before Memorisation

Lessons emphasize understanding the “why” behind ideas before applying formulas, procedures and exam techniques.

02

Guided Practice

Students work through examples and progressively challenging questions with clear feedback and correction.

03

Focused Revision

Revision targets weak areas, common errors and important concepts rather than simply repeating everything.

04

Questions Are Welcome

A supportive learning environment encourages learners to ask questions and explain their reasoning without fear.

05

Progress-Oriented Support

Lessons can be adjusted as the learner develops, with attention to understanding, practice habits and confidence.

06

Convenient Online Learning

Learn from home through Google Meet or Zoom with scheduled individual or small-group sessions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Parents & Learners Often Ask

Are the lessons individual or group-based?

Both options are available. Individual tutoring provides personalized support, while small-group revision offers collaborative practice at a lower per-student rate.

Which online platforms are used?

Lessons can be conducted through Google Meet or Zoom, depending on the agreed arrangement.

Can a learner receive help with a specific difficult topic?

Yes. Sessions can focus on particular concepts, homework, revision gaps or problem-solving skills rather than following a fixed sequence every time.

Do you guarantee a particular grade?

No. The academy provides professional teaching, practice and revision support, but academic outcomes also depend on the learner's effort, attendance, practice and other factors.

Are Grade 9 Mathematics learning materials available?

Yes. Book 1 (Grade 9 Mathematics Revision Book) and Book 2 (Topical Questions & Answers) are now featured in the Learning Resource Centre. Free samples can be downloaded, while full-book enquiries can be made through WhatsApp.

Are Grade 9 Integrated Science revision books available?

Yes. Peak Achievers offers a Grade 9 Integrated Science Revision Book and an Integrated Science Topical Questions & Answers book. Learners can open free PDF samples in the Learning Resource Centre and order the complete digital editions through WhatsApp.

How do I book?

The quickest option is WhatsApp. You can also call, send an SMS or email using the contact details below.
